  Dual-thumb harmonica
  Phone: turn to landscape and raise the volume. Left thumb: slide along the left track. Up-left is draw, down-right is blow, the middle is silent. Slide toward the middle of the screen for vibrato. Right thumb: drag the ribbon past the playhead. Pull outward to widen from the middle hole (up to 5 holes). For a second note, lift the thumb (the note keeps playing), then touch above the ribbon at the hole you want; slide to change it, lift to drop it. Flick and let go and it keeps rolling; touch to stop.
  Gamepad: the left and right pads (or sticks) work like the two thumbs. Triggers open neighbouring holes: left trigger to the left, right trigger to the right (analog, three stages each). D-pad moves along the top row, A edits or accepts, B cancels, Back shows a raw axis readout. Steam Input: set both pads to Joystick Move.
  Tap or press A to start
